    Notes for the designer

    • Keep $889M by itself in the center. The $109M in taxes is already part of it, so don't add the two together or put a sum anywhere.
    • $513.3M and $110.6M are parts reported next to the total. They're not designed to add up to $889M, so don't draw them as slices of a pie or a stacked bar.
    • Keep the source line. It updates automatically to list the source of every figure and quote on each slide. With the recommended figures it reads: "Sources: Visit Seattle (Sept. 22, 2026; analysis by Tourism Economics, part of Oxford Economics); SeattleFWC26 Final Report (Sept. 2026)."
    • Keep the wording that limits each claim: "estimated," "supported," "ticketed," "on match days." Those words are what make the graphic accurate.

    Where the official sources differ

    Jobs

    Visit Seattle says "about 20,000." SeattleFWC26 says "20K+." Use "about 20,000 jobs supported."

    Tax revenue

    Visit Seattle says $109M. SeattleFWC26 says $108.9M. Use $109M. It's the same number, rounded.

    Volunteers

    The Final Report says "2,000+." The Legacy site says "2k." Use "2,000+."

    "Nearly $900M"

    This appears in SeattleFWC26 quotes. Use exactly $889M in SSC's own copy.

    03 · The Story

    Five takeaways the public should remember.

    1. 1

      A greater-than-expected total.

      Visit Seattle forecast $929M in December 2024 and $846M in March 2026. The final estimate is $889M, which Visit Seattle calls a "greater-than-expected total." Visit Seattle ↗

    2. 2

      The world showed up.

      Visit Seattle credits a larger contingent of international fans at matches, more overnight visitors at watch parties than anticipated, and the addition of a second U.S. Men's National Team match. Visit Seattle ↗

    3. 3

      It moved people, not just money.

      The tournament drew 401,400 ticketed fans and more than 1.2M attendees at Seattle fan sites. Four out of five ticketed fans arrived without a personal car, and Link light rail set a one-day record of 309K riders. Final Report ↗

    4. 4

      The legacy outlasts the final whistle.

      More than 35 community and legacy projects, including 52 RAVE Foundation playfields and free tickets and support for more than 1,400 young people and their caregivers. Final Report ↗ Legacy site ↗

    5. 5

      The lessons are part of the win.

      Hospitality results were mixed, and hotel occupancy fell even as revenue rose. SeattleFWC26's lessons, like "just one block makes a big difference in fan traffic," point the way for the next major event. Visit Seattle ↗ Final Report ↗

      07 · Sources

      Where every figure comes from.

      SSC's rule is to stay in lockstep with Visit Seattle on every number. The figures in this kit come only from official publications: Visit Seattle, SeattleFWC26, SSC, FIFA, Sound Transit, King County Metro, and the Downtown Seattle Association. News coverage is used only for press prep.

      Not in this kit: independent or alternative economic models, and partner program data that hasn't been cleared yet. That keeps every public figure consistent with Visit Seattle's official numbers.
